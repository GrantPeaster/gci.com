Putting the Design Back on the Ground.

What Staking Covers
  • Clearing & Grubbing LimitsInitial boundary stakes establishing the construction envelope before earthwork begins.
  • Rough & Fine Grading StakesCut and fill stakes set to grade for mass earthwork and fine grading operations.
  • Utilities & InfrastructureStorm, sewer, water, and other underground infrastructure staked to plan.
  • Building Layout & ControlColumn lines, building corners, and elevation benchmarks set for vertical construction.
  • As-Built VerificationField checks confirming construction matches design before critical pours and placements.

Why GCI for Construction Staking

When GCI surveyed the site and helped engineer the design, our staking crews already know the project. That eliminates the lag that comes when a separate staking firm has to re-familiarize with someone else's plans.

We stake residential, commercial, institutional, and industrial projects across north and central Georgia — and stay on the job through final as-built verification.
