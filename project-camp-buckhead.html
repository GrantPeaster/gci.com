Camp Buckhead — lake lot layout · Buckhead, GA
Challenge

Severe soils ruled out individual septic across the site, and no public sewer or water was accessible within 291 acres — a combination that stops most development before it starts.

Solution

GCI coordinated a community septic system sited with a specialty firm, negotiated an off-site connection to Piedmont Water, and provided weekly construction administration through every phase of build-out.

Result

Successful permitting, 31 delivered lake lots, and a high-end conservation community that the site constraints made impossible without an integrated approach.

Overview

291 Acres. Zero Public Infrastructure.

A conservation subdivision for Thrive More Advisors in Morgan County — 31 lake lots and estate lots carved from a heavily constrained site with no public sewer or water within reach.

Severe soils ruled out individual septic across the site. With no public sewer or water available to serve 291 acres, GCI coordinated a community septic system sited with a specialty firm, negotiated an off-site water connection to Piedmont Water, and provided weekly construction administration through build-out. The result is a cohesive, high-end lake community that wouldn't have been developable without integrated planning from the first site visit.
