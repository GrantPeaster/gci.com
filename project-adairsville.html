Adairsville Logistics Center

ALTA/NSPS Survey Industrial Due Diligence Adairsville, GA
Adairsville Logistics Center — aerial overview · Adairsville, GA
Challenge

A major industrial acquisition required a thorough, lender-grade ALTA/NSPS survey to document boundary, easements, improvements, and encroachments across the full site — on a due diligence timeline.

Solution

GCI prepared a complete ALTA/NSPS land title survey coordinated with title and legal teams — locating all easements of record, visible improvements, and encroachments against the legal boundary, with applicable Table A items addressed.

Result

A clean, lender-accepted survey exhibit that gave the buyer and their title company the confidence to close on a significant industrial acquisition in Bartow County.

Overview

The Survey Lenders and Buyers Rely On.

The Adairsville Logistics Center is a multi-building industrial campus developed by Ashley Capital in Adairsville, Georgia — a growing logistics corridor in Bartow County along the I-75 corridor.

GCI provided the ALTA/NSPS land title survey required to complete the commercial transaction — documenting the legal boundary, all easements and rights-of-way of record, improvements, encroachments, and applicable Table A items. Because our survey team works alongside our planning and engineering principals, the data gathered for due diligence is ready to carry forward into any future site work without starting from scratch.
