Morgan County Recreation Park · Morgan County, GA
Challenge

A blank site with no inherited layout — every field position, access point, utility stub, and future expansion zone had to be resolved from scratch while keeping the phasing logic constructible within a public budget.

Solution

GCI led the layout from site constraints outward, embedding phasing logic into the original design so each phase functions independently and connects cleanly to the next without reconstruction.

Result

A fully operational recreation complex with a phasing structure that allows Morgan County to expand on its own timeline without revisiting foundational infrastructure decisions.

Overview

70 acres planned and laid out from scratch.

A public recreation complex GCI designed from the ground up for Morgan County — fields, facilities, and infrastructure built to expand without revisiting early decisions.

Unlike Prowell Park, there was no inherited plan — GCI originated the layout, working from site constraints outward to position fields, parking, utilities, and future expansion zones. The phasing strategy was embedded into the original design: each phase stands alone, connects cleanly to the next, and avoids the utility conflicts that typically force reconstruction when public facilities grow.
