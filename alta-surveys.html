What ALTA Covers
  • Boundary & Title LinesPrecise legal boundary delineation tied to record documents and monuments.
  • Easements & Rights-of-WayAll recorded and visible easements located and shown on the exhibit.
  • Improvements & EncroachmentsBuildings, parking, utilities, and any improvements documented relative to the boundary.
  • Zoning Setbacks & Flood ZoneApplicable zoning classifications and FEMA flood zone data noted per Table A requirements.
  • Access & UtilitiesPoints of ingress, egress, and utility service locations identified on-site.
