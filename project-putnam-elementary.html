Challenge

A new K–12 facility required precision staking across a complex site — coordinating building layout, parking, utilities, and grading to match an award-winning architectural design and keep construction on schedule.

Solution

GCI provided full construction staking services from clearing limits through building layout and fine grading — working directly with the contractor and coordinating with Breaux and Associates Architects to ensure the design intent was carried precisely into the field.

Result

A facility delivered on grade and on schedule — and recognized by American School & University Magazine with the 2025 Outstanding Design Award. A school the community can point to with pride.

Overview

Putting the Design Back on the Ground.

The Putnam County Elementary School is a new K–12 facility serving the Putnam County School System in Eatonton, Georgia — designed by Breaux and Associates Architects and recognized with the 2025 Outstanding Design Award by American School & University Magazine.

GCI provided construction staking throughout the build — from initial clearing and grubbing limits through rough grading, utility layout, building corners, and fine grading. When GCI is involved in both the survey and staking, the field crew already knows the site. There's no re-familiarization with someone else's plans, and no translation errors between design and construction. The school was completed on schedule and stands as one of the more visible examples of GCI's institutional work in the region.
