Camp Buckhead

291 Acres. Zero Public Infrastructure.

Residential · Thrive More Advisors · Morgan County, GA

291Acres Developed
31Lake Lots Delivered
Challenge

Severe soils ruled out individual septic, with no public sewer or water across 291 acres.

Solution

Community septic siting with a specialty firm, an off-site connection to Piedmont Water, and weekly construction administration.

Result

Successful permitting and a cohesive, high-end 31-lake-lot community.
