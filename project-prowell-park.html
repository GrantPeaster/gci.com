Prowell Park — site plan · Sharpsburg, GA
Challenge

An inherited master plan that resolved the vision but not the buildability — drainage, buffers against adjacent subdivisions, and a phasing sequence that left early phases stranded without later infrastructure.

Solution

GCI reworked grading and drainage to respect adjacencies, resolved buffer conflicts with neighboring subdivisions, and restructured the phasing sequence so each phase functions independently.

Result

A publicly operational park facility with a phasing plan that expands cleanly — no rework required as future phases come online.

Overview

From Inherited Plan to Buildable Facility.

A ~300-acre public park sited between two residential subdivisions — GCI took an existing master plan and made it constructible.

The site came with a conceptual layout that hadn't resolved the hard questions: drainage patterns crossing lot lines, buffer requirements against adjacent neighborhoods, lighting standards, and phasing logic that kept early phases functional while later phases were still years out. GCI reworked the grading, drainage, and phasing sequence to deliver a facility that serves the public from day one and expands without rework.
